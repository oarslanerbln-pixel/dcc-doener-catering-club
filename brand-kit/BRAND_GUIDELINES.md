## 03 / Logo Architecture (`public/brand/`) (F2)

### Emblem: *The Symmetrical Sculpted Yaprak Döner Spit*
Constructed strictly from a centered vertical rotisserie spindle with curved finial crown, 4 strictly symmetrical sculpted cubic-Bézier (`C`) Yaprak Döner tiers tapering cleanly into a curved drip base plate, rendered with a continuous `userSpaceOnUse` Pure White → Champagne Gold (`#D4A853`) → Berlin Emerald (`#2BC48A`) gradient and balanced `"DCC"` monogram beneath (with a crisp 3-tier `simplified` silhouette for `16px–32px` favicons).

## 04 / Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

- **Clear-Space Module (`x`):** Equal to `0.25×` of the emblem outer diameter (`18px` on `logo-primary` `420×108`). Maintain at least `1x` uninterrupted breathing room on all 4 sides.
- **Minimum Reproduction Thresholds:**
  - `logo-primary`: `160px` wide (digital) / `38mm` (print)
  - `logo-stacked`: `110px` wide (digital) / `28mm` (print)
  - `logo-seal`: `48px` diameter (digital) / `18mm` (print)
  - `logo-monogram`: `28px` (digital) / `10mm` (print); dedicated `favicon.svg` simplifies to 3 bold tiers for `16px` crispness.

## 05 / Visual Do's & Don'ts (6 Mandatory Rules)

1. **No Stretching or Skewing:** Always lock the exact `420:108` aspect ratio on `logo-primary` and never distort the symmetrical spit geometry.
2. **No Drop Shadows or Thin Gold Borders:** Never wrap logo cards or buttons in thin gold border outlines or fuzzy CSS drop shadows; keep containers sharp-cornered or frameless.
3. **No Unapproved Gradients:** Never apply neon cyan, pink, or rainbow AI gradients; use Pure White → Champagne Gold (`#D4A853`) → Berlin Emerald (`#2BC48A`).
4. **Never Rotate the Spit Axis:** The vertical skewer rod must remain strictly upright (`0°`), except in deliberate step-and-repeat wrap paper stamps.
5. **Respect Ink Polarity:** Never place `-light` on Light Rechner Ivory/white paper, and never place `-dark` on Pure Black dark surfaces.
6. **Never Invade `1x` Clear Space:** Keep copy, buttons, and borders outside the `1x` protective margin.

## 07 / Typography & DE/EN/TR Diacritic Discipline

Self-hosted in `public/fonts/` with zero external CDN dependencies:
- **Primary Display & UI Sans (`Montserrat`, `500 / 700 / 800`):** Wordmarks, bold white hero display statements, section headings (`letter-spacing: -0.028em`).
- **Architectural Sans (`Space Grotesk`, `300–700`):** Supporting UI copy (`400`, never unintentional `300` default!), subheads (`500–600`).
- **Editorial Serif (`Instrument Serif`, `400` Regular & Italic):** Contrast accents paired on a locked baseline (*"Feuer. Handwerk."*). Never faux-bolded.
- **Technical Mono (`JetBrains Mono`, `100–800`):** Step indices (`01`–`04`), receipt figures, timestamps, and uppercase micro-labels (`+0.12em` to `+0.18em` tracking).
- **Mandatory Tabular Numerals:** All calculator rows, discounts, VAT, and receipt columns apply `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;` (`"tnum" 1, "lnum" 1`).
- **Verified Pangrams (`0` missing glyphs):**
  - DE: *Zwölf Boxkämpfer jagen Viktor quer über den großen Sylter Deich — 19 % MwSt. · € 2.450,00*
  - TR: *Pijamalı hasta yağız şoföre çabucak güvendi — İĞÜŞÖÇ ğüşiöç · ₺ / €*

## 08 / Grid, Safe Zones & Motion Physics

- **12-Column Swiss Grid:** `max-width: 1280px`, `24px` desktop gutter, `16px` mobile gutter, Golden Ratio (`1.618`) spatial splits.
- **Broadcast & Social Safe Zones:**
  - `16:9` Landscape: `5.5%` left/right, `7.5%` top, `8.5%` bottom.
  - `9:16` Portrait (Reels / Stories): `7.5%` left/right, **`14%` top** & **`21%` bottom** kept clear of critical typography/UI.
- **Physical Easing (zero raw `linear` UI motion):**
  - `--ease-expo-out`: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (`450–650ms` entrances)
  - `--ease-quint-in-out`: `cubic-bezier(0.83, 0, 0.17, 1)` (`380–520ms` panel wipes)
  - `--ease-exit-snap`: `cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)` (`220–320ms` exits)
  - `--stagger-step`: `45ms` sequential hierarchy
